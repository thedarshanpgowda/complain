HANDLE REJECTION IN LOVE

Handling rejection in love can be difficult, but it is important to remember that not every relationship will work out. Let them go who didn’t accept your feelings and Focus on Building yourself. You really deserve Better in the Future so TAKE CARE OF YOURSELF yo!. Everyone has their own decisions so respect the decision and don’t try to force anyone, or else you will lose your Self-respect. Don’t Lower your Standards for Anyone.

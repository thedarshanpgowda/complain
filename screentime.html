"Why is it important to reduce screen time?"

Reducing screen time is crucial for maintaining overall well-being. Prolonged screen exposure has been linked to various health issues, including eye strain, disrupted sleep patterns, and sedentary lifestyles. Excessive screen use can hinder physical activity and social interactions, contributing to mental health concerns like anxiety and depression. Moreover, constant digital engagement can adversely affect attention spans and cognitive function. Prioritizing screen time reduction fosters a healthier lifestyle, promoting physical activity, improved sleep, and enhanced interpersonal connections, thereby safeguarding both physical and mental health.

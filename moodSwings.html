How to manage Mood Swings

What are mood swings and how to manage them

  • Experiencing a fluctuation of emotion for long periods is known as a mood swing
  • Some indications that you might be going through mood swings are extreme low moods for an extended period of more than two weeks, signs of feeling ups and downs, and the inability to explain why you feel a certain way.

There's no such thing as good people or bad people, there are just people who do wrong things.

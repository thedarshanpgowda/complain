Why Do We Need Sleep?

Sleep allows your body and mind to recharge, leaving you refreshed and alert when you wake up. Healthy sleep also helps the body remain healthy and stave off diseases. Without enough sleep, the brain cannot function properly. This can impare your abilities to concentrate, think clearly, and process memories.Most adults require more tha seven hours of sleep.

Steps toimprove your sleep habits.

  • Minimize Noise
  • Block Out Light
  • Set your alarm for the same time each day
  • Keep Naps around 20 minutes
  • Disconnect devices in the hour before bed
  • Exercise for at least 20 minutes each day
  • Eat dinner a few hours before bed
  • Limit nicotine use and alcohol
  • Reserve your bed only for sleep
